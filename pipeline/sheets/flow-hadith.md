# FLOW: Hadith checking flow / مسار فحص الحديث

## INTAKE
For every claim of kind «hadith» the extractor hands the checker: (1) the speaker's words verbatim as spoken, dialect kept, with start and end time in the video; (2) to whom the speaker attributed them: the Prophet ﷺ, Allah as a hadith qudsi (words the Prophet ﷺ relays from his Lord that are not Qur'an), a Companion or Successor, or just «a hadith» with no name; (3) any reference, narrator or grade the speaker named («in Bukhari», «from Abu Hurayra», «a weak hadith», «a sahih hadith», «a fabricated hadith»), and whether he told it affirmatively («the Prophet said») or in a hedging form («it is narrated», «it is said»); (4) whether he gave it word for word, by meaning or in translation; (5) the video's language; (6) any commentary of the speaker fused with the text; (7) one claim per hadith: several hadiths in a row are several claims; a verse fused with a hadith is split; a legal ruling the speaker draws from the hadith is a separate claim of kind «ruling». The extractor hands over no grade of its own and no source from memory; source and grade come from the searches in the steps below, never from the model's knowledge.

## THE STEPS AND GATES, IN ORDER (a gate has yes/no targets; a target is a step id or an exit id)
1. [s1] STEP: Separate the attributed text from the speaker's own commentary and record: to whom it was attributed (the Prophet ﷺ / Allah as a hadith qudsi / a Companion / a Successor / «a hadith» with no name), whether it was given word for word, by meaning or in translation, affirmatively or hedged, and any reference, narrator or grade the speaker named. Record too whether the speaker's claim is itself a grading («this hadith is fabricated») rather than a telling. The speaker's words are never altered; they are what the viewer sees in the «What was said» card. (note: If the speaker's commentary is fused with the text so a viewer might take it for part of the hadith, the whole of what he said stays in «What was said»; only the attributed part is judged. The speaker's understanding of the hadith and his use of it as evidence are not judged in this flow: the grade is on the transmission only, and the app never rules. The checker's reasoning stays with the moderator.) → next: g1
2. [g1] GATE: Is the attributed text a Qur'anic verse, or part of one? (note: Check by an exact phrase on quran.com before deciding; the model's memory is not evidence. A hadith qudsi is not Qur'an and is not handed over. A claim ABOUT a verse — its virtue, when to recite it — is not a verse: «Ayat al-Kursi before sleep keeps Satan away» is a hadith, even when the extractor wrote the verse key 2:255 as its reference.) → yes: x_rekind_quran · no: g2
3. [g2] GATE: Is the text attributed to the Prophet ﷺ, to Allah as a hadith qudsi, to a Companion or Successor as a transmitted report (an athar), or did the speaker just call it «a hadith» with no name? (note: An athar is a report of a Companion's or Successor's words or deeds. Words attributed to the Prophet ﷺ are «marfu'» (raised to him), to a Companion «mawquf» (stopped at him), to a Successor «maqtu'». All run through this flow, because dorar's encyclopaedia grades them all. «A hadith» with no name means, in ordinary usage, from the Prophet ﷺ.) → yes: s2 · no: g2b
4. [g2b] GATE: Is there a specific text to look up at all? (note: «There are many hadiths on the virtue of patience» is not a claim of a specific hadith. «The Prophet ﷺ used to pray two rak'as before Fajr» is one, searched by its meaning.) → yes: s2 (the search runs FIRST, whatever the attribution; g2c is asked only if the search then finds nothing, at g3c) · no: x_not_a_claim
5. [g2c] GATE: Did the speaker himself attribute the words to a named later person (a scholar, an imam, a sage)? (note: «Yes» = the extractor mis-kinded it; hand to the saying flow. «No» = a story or report about earlier peoples or history with no narrator → the history flow. Discovering after a search that a «hadith» is a scholar's saying happens later, at g3d.) → yes: x_rekind_saying · no: x_rekind_history
6. [s2] STEP: Search dorar.net's hadith encyclopaedia WITH THE TOOL, for EVERY claim of kind «hadith» that names a specific text, whatever it is attributed to (the Prophet ﷺ, Allah as a hadith qudsi, a Companion such as Bilal speaking inside the Prophet's ﷺ story, a Successor, «a hadith» with no name, or nobody) and whatever its reference says (a verse key on a claim about a verse's virtue is no reason to skip). (a) Write two or three SHORT queries of four to six distinctive words each, in the hadith's KNOWN classical wording, not the speaker's dialect (he said «اللي يقرأها قبل النوم»; the hadith says «إذا أويت إلى فراشك فاقرأ آية الكرسي»); a narrator or collection the speaker named may be one word of a query. (b) Run them one at a time: node "{TOOLS}/dorar.mjs" search "<words>" --tag <videoId>#<i>; stop at the first query whose hits carry the narration meant. dorar requires EVERY word of the query inside one narration, so four words of the known wording find what ten words of a paraphrase miss. (c) If the tool prints «STATUS blocked» (it has already retried four times), this claim ends at x_pending_lookup: blocked is never «not found», and the gap is never filled from memory. (note: No hadith is placed in a collection from the model's memory; a proposed wording is not evidence until it appears in a hit the tool printed, and the record's link must be a permalink the tool printed (today's rule: a permalink must be a fetched hit). Every search lands in the tool's ledger; a result whose dorar link is not in the ledger is refused by check-dorar-links.mjs. Measured 1 Oct: 5 of 5 claims the long pipeline query had missed resolved with four to six words of the known wording.) → next: g3
7. [g3] GATE: Did any hit carry the same meaning as the attributed text? → yes: s4 · no: s3_retry
8. [s3_retry] STEP: Only after THREE queries in the known wording have come back empty (empty, not blocked): look ONCE in the Kuwaiti encyclopaedia for a printed wording of the hadith — node "{TOOLS}/usul-online.mjs" kuwaiti-encyclopedia-of-jurisprudence find "<three to five words of its meaning>" — since its entries often print the wording with its takhrij; then run ONE more dorar search with four to six words of that printed wording. The encyclopaedia supplies words to search with, never the source: the record's source is a dorar hit or nothing. Phrases people repeat that are not hadith come back from the same search with a «no basis» or «not a hadith» grading (dorar's widespread-sayings works, e.g. Kashf al-Khafa'). → next: g3b
9. [g3b] GATE: Found now in a hadith collection? → yes: s4 · no: g3c
10. [g3c] GATE: Does an accepted hadith scholar (or dorar's widespread-sayings section) state of these words «no basis», «not a hadith» or «fabricated»? (note: «No basis» means it exists in no hadith book with any chain at all, stronger than our merely not finding it. A scholar's «I did not find it» is not that judgment; it travels to the not-found exit as a line supporting our search. Whether dorar's own statement suffices, or a named scholar is required, is an open question for the shaykh.) → yes: g3d · no: x_notfound (but when g2 answered «no» — the words were the speaker's own story or a later person's — go to g2c instead: the re-kind now comes after the search, never instead of it)
11. [g3d] GATE: Does that statement name the true author among later scholars or sages («these are in fact so-and-so's words»)? (note: Most of the widespread-sayings section reads like this: «not a hadith; these are al-Hasan al-Basri's words». If a Companion is named, the words are mawquf and go back to s2, not handed over.) → yes: x_rekind_saying · no: x_baseless
12. [s4] STEP: Pin the source in this order: (a) the hit whose wording is closest to the speaker's, at the level he attributed (marfu' or mawquf); (b) among the hits that carry that narration, THE FIXED ORDER OF PREFERENCE (the owner, 2 Oct 2026): tier 1 Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim; tier 2 al-Albani's grading books (Sahih/Da'if al-Jami', Sahih/Da'if al-Targhib, Sahih/Da'if of the Sunan, al-Silsila al-Sahiha/al-Da'ifa, Irwa' al-Ghalil, Ghayat al-Maram); tier 3 Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut's gradings (the Musnad, the Sunan, Ibn Hibban); tier 4 Ibn Hajar's grading works. The tool prints each hit's tier. A side book that only mentions the hadith with a passing remark on one chain or narrator (al-Matjar al-Rabih, Dhakhirat al-Huffaz, Majma' al-Zawa'id, Fath al-Bari, Lisan al-Mizan, Mizan al-I'tidal, al-Jami' al-Saghir, al-Tarikh al-Kabir…) and a scholar's fatwa or lesson NEVER give the grade; they may travel only as extra mentions. The same hadith from another tier 1–4 entry counts as found (Bukhari 554 for Bukhari 7434). If the same hadith (same meaning) is in the two Sahihs in another wording, record that as a second card, and do not call the speaker's words «by meaning» when they are another collection's wording. If the words exist both marfu' and mawquf at different grades, pin the hit at the level the speaker claimed and show the other as a second card. Record the permanent link dorar.net/h/… as shown, the hadith text exactly as the site gives it (if long, the passage carrying the speaker's words with «…» at the cuts, never a summary), the narrator and his kind, and whether the text is marfu', mawquf or maqtu'. Do not prefix «the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said» when the matn already begins with it. (c) A hit whose «grading» field only describes (e.g. «أخرجه في صحيحه», «رجاله ثقات») carries no grade; prefer a hit graded by an accepted grader (g5's list) or from the two Sahihs. dorar's takhrij line (e.g. «أخرجه البخاري (2311)») says a Sahih source exists: search or open it (node "{TOOLS}/dorar.mjs" open <permalink>) to pin it as the second card; never cite a collection and number from a takhrij line alone. (d) Fill the part's source object by running node "{TOOLS}/dorar.mjs" cite <permalink> --tag <videoId>#<i> and copying what it prints (link, quote, collection, number, grader, grading, narrator); the cite command refuses a link no search printed. (note: The grade follows the wording and its chain, not the meaning: a Tirmidhi wording may be hasan while the same hadith in Bukhari is sahih; an addition found only in a weak riwaya does not become sahih because the hadith's core is in Bukhari.) → next: g4a
13. [g4a] GATE: Does the speaker's text include a portion the pinned hit lacks (an addition from another riwaya, or a second hadith joined to the first)? → yes: s4b · no: g4
14. [s4b] STEP: Split the claim into parts: one for the hadith's core, one for each addition or second hadith. Search each part alone (s2) and pin its source (s4); each part then continues from g4 at its own grade. The viewer sees every part with its own Source card under the same row. A part with no source takes the not-found exit; it is never dropped and never pulls the other parts down. (note: This applies the decomposition decision (5): in the measurements, the part that failed was usually the added one. If the joining changes the meaning, the flow reaches «corrected» through g8, not here.) → next: g4
15. [g4] GATE: Is the hadith in Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim as a connected hadith (with its full chain; not suspended in a chapter heading — «suspended» means the start of the chain is omitted — and not in Muslim's introduction)? (note: The umma received the two Sahihs with acceptance, so a connected hadith in either is sahih with no grader needed; this is Ibn al-Salah's rule, a candidate for the rulebook. Grade here: sahih. Ibn al-Salah excepted the few hadiths some critics disputed; if a hit carries an accepted grader's differing word on a Sahih hadith, both are shown (s5). Bukhari's affirmative-form suspended hadiths have their own standing in Ibn Hajar; an open question.) → yes: s5 · no: g5
16. [g5] GATE: Is the pinned hit a tier 2–4 entry (s4's fixed order: al-Albani's grading books, Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut's gradings, Ibn Hajar's grading works) that grades the hadith itself? (Since 2 Oct the tiers ARE the accepted graders list; a side book or a fatwa never grades; widening the list is the shaykh's call) (note: A description of the narrators alone («its narrators are trustworthy», «its narrators are those of the Sahih») is not a grade of the hadith; the chain may be broken or the text anomalous. It does not count.) → yes: s5 · no: x_ungraded
17. [s5] STEP: Fix the grade from the grader's own word, never from your reading (a connected Sahih hadith: sahih by the g4 rule). Sahih: «sahih», «sahih li-ghayrihi», «its chain is sahih». Hasan: «hasan», «hasan li-ghayrihi», «its chain is hasan», and «hasan sahih» (as caution: Ibn Hajar reads it, for a single-chain hadith, as hesitation between the two grades). Weak: «weak», «weak chain», «very weak», «munkar» (a weak narrator's report that contradicts the reliable ones), and «does not hold» on its own. Fabricated: «fabricated», «false», «no basis». If two accepted graders differ, rank at the weaker and show both verbatim with both names — except that the fabricated level is never given when another accepted grader graded it hasan or sahih; then it drops to weak with both grades shown. The grader's exact word always travels to the Source card: source.grading holds the page's word ALONE, exactly as dorar prints it (any note of yours goes to reason_ar/reason_en, never into the grading), and when graders differ EACH grader is its own entry in source.gradings ({grader, grading, link, collection, number}, each copied from a cite of that grader's own permalink), never several graders packed into one field. (note: The «weaker when graders differ» rule and the «no fabricated level against an accepted hasan» rule are ours until the shaykh confirms or replaces them. The grade is never the checker's own.) → next: g6
18. [g6] GATE: Is the grade fabricated, false or baseless? (note: Fabricated (mawdu') is an invented lie attributed to the Prophet ﷺ. «Munkar», «very weak» and «does not hold» are not fabricated; they stay under weak with the grader's word.) → yes: g6b · no: g8
19. [g6b] GATE: Did the speaker himself say this hadith is fabricated, unfounded or baseless (his claim being a grading, not a telling)? → yes: x_mawdu_stated · no: x_mawdu
20. [g8] GATE: Does the speaker's wording keep the source's meaning? (Yes = word for word, or faithfully by meaning, or a portion cut without changing the meaning. No = a clause added that is in no riwaya, a condition or exception dropped, the meaning reversed, two hadiths merged as one text so the meaning changes, or a cut where the meaning turns) (note: Narrating by meaning is giving the hadith in other words while keeping its meaning; most scholars allowed it for one who knows the meanings. A colloquial rendering with the same meaning is narrating by meaning, not distortion. An addition found in another riwaya is not this case; it was split at s4b.) → yes: g7 · no: x_misworded
21. [g7] GATE: Did the speaker attribute the words to the same level as the source? («No» = a Companion's or Successor's words that the speaker told as the Prophet's ﷺ, and no marfu' hit exists) (note: If a marfu' hit exists, it was pinned at s4 at its own grade and passes here with «yes». The reverse (the Prophet's ﷺ words told as a Companion's own) is a detail note at s6, not a correction; the words are supported, and more.) → yes: s6 · no: x_mawquf_as_marfu
22. [s6] STEP: Record the detail notes that will appear in the «Difference» slot without changing the colour: quoted by meaning; a portion of the hadith; another collection's wording while the Sahihs have it in other words; the speaker named a collection or narrator other than the source's; the speaker stated a grade the graders contradict; the Prophet's ﷺ words told as a Companion's. Each note is one sentence of fact, no reasoning. → next: g9
23. [g9] GATE: Is the grade sahih? → yes: x_sahih · no: g10
24. [g10] GATE: Is the grade hasan? → yes: x_hasan · no: g11
25. [g11] GATE: Did the speaker himself say the hadith is weak, or tell it in a hedging form («it is narrated», «it is said», «it has reached us»)? (note: A hedging form is a phrase that signals the report is not established, such as «it is narrated» or «it is mentioned»; the scholars prescribed it when telling a weak hadith without its chain.) → yes: x_daif_stated · no: x_daif_as_accepted

- source slot: «{hadith_text}» as the encyclopaedia carries it — {collection_or_work} {number} · Fabricated (mawdu'): «{grading}» · graded by {grader}[ · {grader_2}: «{grading_2}»] · dorar.net ↗ {permalink}. (The grader's exact word is kept.)
- difference slot (EN): {grader}[ and {grader_2}] graded this hadith «{grading}»: in his judgment its attribution to the Prophet ﷺ is not established. The scholars ruled that such a report may be told only together with a statement of its status; that statement is what is shown here.

### x_ungraded — state not_found · level 0 · publishable — Found in a collection, no accepted grading / حديث وُجد في كتابٍ بلا حكم من محدِّث معتمَد
