# The unreviewed-book note (owner, 5 Oct 2026)

**Verified books show no note:** the nine verified copies, plus three the owner approved on 5 Oct while reviewing the library clips:
- Ibn Uthaymin, *Risala fi al-qada' wa-l-qadar* (usul.ai `a-treatise-on-predestination-and-fate`)
- al-Nawawi, *Riyad al-Salihin* (usul.ai `riyad-salihin`, ed. Mahir Yasin al-Fahl, Dar Ibn Kathir 1428)
- Abu Nu'aym, *Hilyat al-Awliya* (usul.ai `hilyat-awliya`, Matba'at al-Sa'ada)

**Any other book shows the note** (al-Hikam al-Ata'iyya is the first), version C chosen from three (https://claude.ai/artifact/VM2XGCEKXTcGermHhN76DQ):
- label: «كتابٌ لم يراجعه مختصٌّ بعد» / "Book not yet reviewed by a specialist"
- on tap: «وجدنا العبارة في هذا الكتاب، ووجودُها فيه لا يعني صحةَ مضمونها، ولا اتفاقَ العلماء عليه.» / "We found these words in this book. Being in it does not mean the content is sound, or that scholars agree on it."

The owner's reason, in his words: «ثبوت وجود العبارة في الكتاب لا يعني صحة مضمونها شرعًا أو اتفاق العلماء عليه», and the note must say the book has not been reviewed by a specialist yet.

## When the note appears (the owner's clarification, 5 Oct)

The note appears ONLY when the book's verification was not confirmed during the review of the sources:
- the reviewer CONFIRMS the book -> it joins the verified list, no note (and it is not asked again);
- the reviewer REFUSES the book -> the part goes to "not found" (the 4 Oct rule);
- the reviewer does neither -> the line may be published, WITH the note.
On the library clips: the Risala, Riyad al-Salihin and Hilyat al-Awliya were confirmed (no note); al-Hikam al-Ata'iyya was not (note).
