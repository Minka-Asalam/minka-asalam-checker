Claims Pipeline Design

From a video to a reviewed record, designed from scratch on everything we built and measured before. Each stage below shows the owner's decision, who does each step, and what has been measured. One principle runs through it: the script holds the steps, a model answers one small question at a time, and the costly agent is only the fallback.

Find the source and its level

Verses

The script alone

Measured

The script: finds the verse on quran.com from the words or the reference, compares word by word, verse ranges included. One letter off → a moderator listens. More off → one small question to a model: a different reading, a slip, or a translation?

a simple search found 26 of 26 · cost ≈ 0

Hadith · the owner's tree

Haiku first, Opus for what it cannot finish

Measured

Haiku: "Where can I find it?" · "Which of these did he mean?" · "Say it another way". The script: searches dorar from the PC, ranks what dorar printed, copies every detail, sets the level from the grading. Opus: the same tree again when Haiku finds nothing or picks a weak narration while a sound one was printed. The big checker: only if Opus also fails.

64 of 64 the same hadith as approved · no wrong hadith published · ≈ 1.3¢ a hadith · today's checker ≈ 7¢

Rulings, reports, fatwas, figures

Split first, then Opus asks where, the script searches books and fatwas, Haiku judges

Adopted 4 Oct

Sonnet, first: how many rulings are in this sentence? One ruling, a general rule plus the case he applies it to, or two rulings packed together; each part gets the plain question a person would ask. Opus: per part, "which book, which chapter, and three sentences in the book's own words, each naming the thing ruled on". The script: searches the verified copies, and searches each plain question on islamweb and islamqa, opening the top five fatwas. Haiku: "which passage states this part: the same, partly, more than the source, the opposite, or none?" Opus's second look: reads the whole page. Opus again only where Haiku found nothing or the second look said no, and Sonnet checks Opus's pick. A page that says the opposite is a found source, shown as a correction. Figures are calculated by the script; a usul.ai book outside the nine is allowed but flagged for the owner.

three reviewed videos: on the 35 rulings both searched, a page for 35 each (fully 14 against today's 15; 3 more found by the new way that today's checker left unsearched under a deadline) · Opus needed again on 4 of 63 parts · ≈ 10¢ a ruling · the owner's four fixes (split, ask as a question, name the key term, "the opposite") took it from 14 to 19 on the first 19, then 19 of 19 on unseen ones

5

Second look

Script checks, then a different model's judgement

Decided

The script: every link printed by a search, every quote on its page, every grade as dorar shows it, the speaker's words copied exactly, the whole ladder tried before "not found", the state the worst of its parts. A different model from the one that picked: does the source say what he said? Is the difference line fair to the speaker and natural Arabic?

already proven in part: on rulings the page check caught every wrong page (12 of 12, then 4 of 4, then 4 in the three-video test, each re-judged by Opus); on hadith it caught Haiku's one wrong pick · to test: would it have caught the sceptic's 8 changes of 1 October?

7

Review and write back

"What needs you" first

Decided

One permanent page builder. Flagged parts first, each with its reason (Opus took over, a weak hadith held, "partly" or "more than the book", a book outside the nine, a letter off in a verse, a long story sourced whole). The rest in clean groups the owner scans and accepts with one tap; every item stays his decision. The page times the review. Write-back as today, guarded.
