# When hadith graders differ — the rule the owner chose (5 Oct 2026), for the shaykh to confirm

**Decided by the owner, pending the shaykh's confirmation.** It replaces the written rule "rank at the weaker" for cases B and C only. The app never chooses between scholars: it reports the graders, each named with their own words.

| Case | What the app shows | Basis (checked in the books) |
|---|---|---|
| **A.** Every accepted grader accepts it (any mix of sahih and hasan) | Accepted; the badge shows the LOWER rank; each grader named with his own words | Ibn Hajar: hasan "shares with sahih in being used as proof, though below it" — [Nuzhat al-Nazar p. 120](https://usul.ai/t/nuzhat-al-nazar-fi-tawdih-nukhabat-al-fikar-fi-mustalah-ahl-al-athar/74?versionId=mQgFnFvZJ1) |
| **B.** One grades the hadith; another only remarks on one chain ("its chain is weak") | The grade on the HADITH sets the badge; the chain remark is a note under it | al-Nawawi: say "weak by this chain", never call the text weak for that chain alone, unless a leading scholar says it has no sound route — [Tadrib al-Rawi 1/348](https://usul.ai/t/tadrib-al-rawi-fi-sharh-taqrib-al-nawawi/326?versionId=_JvaKFVIA0); Ibn al-Salah: "its chain is sound" is less than "the hadith is sound" — [Ulum al-Hadith p. 109](https://usul.ai/t/muqaddimat-culum-hadith/97?versionId=pSG2xZPvCa) |
| **C.** One accepts the hadith itself, another rejects it | A **"graders differ"** badge, both named with their words, NO verdict from the app; flagged on the review page for the owner and the shaykh | al-Nawawi allows later grading only "for one who is capable and strong in knowledge" — [Tadrib al-Rawi 1/157](https://usul.ai/t/tadrib-al-rawi-fi-sharh-taqrib-al-nawawi/135?versionId=_JvaKFVIA0); the brand rule: the app never claims to authenticate |

Also read, as context for caution (not used as the rule): Ibn al-Salah puts criticism before praise when NARRATOR critics conflict — [Ulum al-Hadith p. 221](https://usul.ai/t/muqaddimat-culum-hadith/209?versionId=pSG2xZPvCa) (about narrators, not whole-hadith verdicts; later scholars limited it to criticism that gives its reason, from memory).

**Questions for the shaykh**
1. Is case A right: sahih against hasan is agreement on acceptance, shown at the lower rank?
2. Case B: when al-Arna'ut writes "its chain is weak" and al-Albani grades the hadith hasan, does the hadith's grade lead? (Live example: the Sa'd hadith, pkH5vlUGc5U at 03:32, shown today as "Weak".)
3. Case C: is "graders differ, both named, no verdict" acceptable, or should the app lean one way?
4. What should count as a grade on the chain alone (e.g. «إسناده ضعيف», «رجاله ثقات»)?

**What it changes once confirmed:** the hadith tree's record step (method-v4 chain6.mjs: today it applies "rank at the weaker" and flags "graders differ"), the checker's written rule s5, and a new "graders differ" badge in the display (a mockup first). The live record's rows that fall under B or C are re-checked.

Correction of an earlier chat answer: Ibn Hajar's own commentary on the Nukhba is *Nuzhat al-Nazar* (not *Fath al-Mughith*, which is al-Sakhawi's commentary on al-'Iraqi's Alfiyya); the "al-Tahrir fi Mustalahat al-Hadith" by al-Jurjani could not be confirmed.
