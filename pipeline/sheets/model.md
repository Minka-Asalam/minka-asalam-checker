# THE RECORD MODEL (live reference tables, migration 218). Data, not instructions.

## STATES (what the comparison found). tone: null = the level decides.
- by_meaning — by meaning / بالمعنى — tone (level) — the sense is the source's, the wording is not: a translation, a paraphrase told as such, a reference without recitation
- corrected — corrected / مُصحَّح — tone corrected — the source contradicts what was said: a changed meaning, a reversed attribution, a wrong book, a different figure, a dropped condition, a verse cut where the meaning inverts
- matches — matches / مطابق — tone (level) — the words or the position are as the source has them
- not_a_claim — not a claim / ليس ادعاءً — tone neutral — never published
- not_checked — not checked / لا نفحصه — tone neutral — a source outside our list, or a worldly figure our sources cannot carry
- not_found — not found / لم يُوجد — tone notfound — we searched the cleared sources and did not find the part
- overstated — overstated / مبالَغ في درجته — tone (level) — the speaker's word for the degree of agreement is stronger than the source states, while the position itself is the source's
- pending — pending / معلّق — tone neutral — the source did not answer; try again
- rekinded — re-kinded / يُحوَّل إلى نوع آخر — tone neutral — handed to another kind's flow

## LEVELS per kind (strength of the source found; 0 = none). Colour tone in brackets.
### quran
- level 0 [notfound] — No source / لا مصدر: No source found, nothing to rank; for exits not yet resolved, handed to another kind's flow, or not a claim at all.
- level 1 [strong] — Mushaf text / نص المصحف: The mushaf text transmitted by tawatur, in any of the ten readings; the displayed text is Hafs as in the Madina mushaf and quran.com. Al-Suyuti's al-Itqan on compilation and preservation; Ibn al-Jazari's al-Nashr on the readings and their three conditions. A closed text, so the top of the table.
- level 2 [sound] — Tafsirs and adopted translations / التفاسير والترجمات المعتمدة: The verse's sense as the cleared tafsirs give it (al-Tabari, al-Baghawi) or as an adopted translation renders it. Scholars agree a translation or a rendering by meaning is an explanation of the Qur'an, not Qur'an (al-Zurqani, Manahil al-'Irfan; the Kuwaiti encyclopaedia «ترجمة»); so it is a source acted on but below the text itself: a report a cleared work carries, an adopted translation.
- level 3 [corrected] — Not in the Qur'an / ليس في القرآن: The Qur'an is complete and closed by tawatur; what is in neither the mushaf nor one of the ten readings is not Qur'an (al-Itqan; al-Nashr; dorar.net on preservation). The bottom rung exists because the mushaf itself gives that judgment.
### hadith
- level 0 [notfound] — No source / لا مصدر: No source for the part in the cleared sources, or its text found in a collection with no accepted grader's ruling on it, or not checked because it belongs to another flow or is not a claim at all. Nothing to rank on the scale. Its colour comes from the state (notfound / neutral), not from this level.
- level 1 [strong] — Authentic (sahih) / صحيح: A report with a continuous chain of upright, precise narrators to its end, free of anomaly and hidden defect (Ibn al-Salah's Muqaddima; Ibn Hajar's Nuzhat al-Nazar). A connected hadith of the two Sahihs is sahih by the umma's acceptance, with no grader needed. The grade is an accepted grader's own word as dorar.net relays it, never the checker's. The top of the scale: an accepted text or the top of the graders' scholarly grading.
- level 2 [sound] — Sound (hasan) / حسن: A continuous chain through upright narrators of slightly lesser precision, free of anomaly and defect (Ibn Hajar); the majority of scholars use it as evidence like the sahih though below it in strength (Ibn al-Salah, the second type; al-Khattabi in the introduction to Ma'alim al-Sunan). Includes «hasan li-ghayrihi» and «its chain is hasan» in the grader's word, and «hasan sahih» as caution (Ibn Hajar reads it, for a single chain, as hesitation). Evidence scholars act on, below the top.
- level 3 [neutral] — Graders differ / اختلف فيه المحدِّثون: A hadith on which the accepted graders split: some graded it hasan or sahih, others weak, each in his own word. Disagreement among the specialists is a recognised state in this science, shown with both grades and never chosen between (Ibn al-Salah on a report's grade varying with its routes and the critics' views). A marker, not a strength: neither acceptance nor weakness.
- level 4 [weak] — Weak (da'if) / ضعيف: What lacks a condition of hasan (Ibn al-Salah). Weakness is a judgment on the chain, not that the meaning is false; Ibn al-Salah states that «not sahih» is no assertion of falsehood in fact. Covers the grader's own «weak», «weak chain», «very weak», «munkar» or «does not hold» alone, kept as is; acting on it is a disagreement among scholars shown in «How to receive it» without choosing, and Ibn Hajar's three conditions separate light from severe weakness.
- level 5 [corrected] — Fabricated or baseless / موضوع أو لا أصل له: Fabricated (mawdu') is an invented lie attributed to the Prophet ﷺ, the worst of the weak, and may only be relayed with a statement of its status (Ibn al-Salah, the twenty-first type). «No basis» is what exists in no hadith book with any chain. Neither is a hadith, so this bottom rung says: not the text. The judgment is always an accepted grader's own word («fabricated», «false», «no basis» — never «does not hold» alone) or from the works on widespread unestablished sayings (al-Sakhawi's al-Maqasid al-Hasana, al-'Ajluni's Kashf al-Khafa', al-Albani's al-Da'ifa). Never given when another accepted grader graded it hasan or sahih. Whether it is published at all is the owner's open decision, not made here.
### ruling
- level 0 [notfound] — No source / لا مصدر: No source for the spoken part in the cleared sources, or not examined yet (pending, handed to another flow, not a claim). Not a strength and nothing to rank; the colour comes from the state: not_found in its own colour, everything else neutral.
- level 1 [strong] — Agreed upon / متفق عليه: Consensus, the Muslim scholars' agreement on the ruling, documented from its reporting books: Ibn al-Mundhir's al-Ijma', Ibn Hazm's Maratib al-Ijma' with Ibn Taymiyya's critique; or the four schools' agreement in the page's own words (the Kuwaiti Encyclopaedia's «the jurists agreed», Ibn Qudama's «we know of no difference»), provisionally, until the shaykh rules. Step one of dorar.net's method. The page's own phrase is shown. The top of the scale, so the tone is strong.
- level 2 [sound] — Majority view / قول الجمهور: The majority, most jurists: three of the four schools, or the page's own word «the majority», with the dissenter named. dorar.net's step two: the preponderant view with the majority. Evidence scholars act on, below consensus, so the tone is sound.
- level 3 [neutral] — Recognised difference / خلاف معتبر: The schools divided, the spoken position the adopted view of two schools with the rest differing; the page's «the jurists differed». A recognised difference, neither strength nor weakness, so the tone is neutral. One level whether the difference was named or not; that lives in the difference slot and the internal severity, not the colour.
- level 4 [neutral] — One school's view / قول مذهب: The adopted position of one school (the view the school gives as its own), or a view within a school where the page names no adopted view. dorar.net's attribution to the adopted view. One school's recognised position, so the tone is neutral.
- level 5 [weak] — Non-adopted view / قول غير معتمد: A non-adopted transmitted view or aspect within a school, or a named scholar's view outside the four schools. dorar.net drops the anomalous; we show it in its page's words, attributed to its holder. Both cases one rung, since no scholarly ordering separates them; the tone is weak.
### fatwa
- level 0 [notfound] — No source / لا مصدر: Nothing in the fatwas and cleared books to rank: no answer to the question, an answer to his premise but not his case, or no body stating his position. Not a strength; the colour here comes from the state (not found, not checked, pending, re-kinded, not a claim).
- level 1 [sound] — Bodies agree / اتفاق الهيئات: Every cleared body takes one position. Collective ijtihad of the fiqh academies and standing committees: stronger than an individual's fatwa, but not consensus, so it ranks sound, not strong. Candidates: dorar.net's method for contemporary matters, the Kuwaiti encyclopaedia «ijtihad», the OIC academy's resolutions on ijtihad and on fatwa.
- level 2 [sound] — One body's fatwa / فتوى هيئة واحدة: One fatwa from a cleared body, with no dissent among the cleared bodies. Rooted in the permission for one who cannot derive rulings himself (taqlid) to act on the fatwa of a scholar he trusts. In the corrected exits the body's own fatwa is the correcting source, so it sits at this level. Candidates: al-Nawawi's Adab al-Fatwa, Ibn al-Salah's Adab al-Mufti.
- level 3 [neutral] — Bodies differ / الهيئات تختلف: The cleared bodies take several positions and his is one of them. Recognised difference among muftis, each position with a basis in evidence and a trusted holder. A marker of difference, not a strength, so its tone is neutral. How he told it (as the only ruling, or with a word of consensus) never changes the tone; it changes the state or the internal severity. Candidates: al-Nawawi, al-Shatibi's al-Muwafaqat, the Kuwaiti «taqlid» and «ikhtilaf».
- level 4 [weak] — A fatwa its body later revised / فتوى عادت عنها الهيئة: A fatwa a cleared body issued and later ruled against, and no cleared body states it today. Rooted in the chapter on the mufti's retraction: the later fatwa is acted on and the earlier one is shown as abandoned. The lowest source strength in this kind; there is no 'not the text' rung here because a fatwa is not a text to be affirmed or denied. Candidates: Ibn al-Salah's Adab al-Mufti (the mufti's retraction), al-Nawawi's Adab al-Fatwa, the Kuwaiti «fatwa».
### report
- level 0 [notfound] — No source / لا مصدر: Nothing found to rank; the state alone carries the row (not found, not checked, pending, rekinded, not a claim).
- level 1 [strong] — Established / ثابت: A sahih chain by a recognised grader (hadith terminology as dorar's graders apply it), or in Bukhari/Muslim, or a basic sirah fact its works state as undisputed (the standard's level A); and a scholar's words in his own work, whose basis is a secure attribution rather than a chain.
- level 2 [sound] — Hasan (a sound chain) / حسن: A recognised grader ruled the chain hasan, in itself or by support; hadith terminology: below sahih, and acted upon. The one table places it at sound, not strong.
- level 3 [sound] — Reported in the sources / مرويّ في المصادر: Found in a cleared sirah, history or tafsir work with its chain or attribution, and no grading of the chain found by the accepted graders; the historians' method of transmission as described by al-Khatib al-Baghdadi, al-Sakhawi and Ibn Khaldun.
- level 4 [neutral] — Unsettled / غير محسوم: A marker, not a strength: the sources carry differing accounts, or the recognised graders differ on the chain, or the source marks it as Israiliyyat, which are neither confirmed nor denied; the difference or the marker is shown as it stands, with no preference, whatever work carries it.
- level 5 [weak] — Weak / ضعيف: A recognised grader ruled the chain weak in any of its degrees (weak, very weak, munkar, wahin); hadith terminology, with the scholars' difference on relaying weak reports in stories and virtues.
- level 6 [corrected] — No basis / لا أصل له: A recognised grader ruled it fabricated, baseless, false or a lie; the bottom rung, a level because the graders give that judgment; hadith terminology on the fabricated, and the prohibition on relaying it undeclared.
### number
- level 0 [notfound] — No source / لا مصدر: No source was found for the part, so there is nothing to rank: not found in the cleared sources, outside them (a worldly statistic), not a claim, or handed to another flow. The colour comes from the state, not the level; the 'neutral' below is a schema filler, not a displayed colour.
- level 1 [strong] — In the text / في النصّ: The figure is stated in a verse of the mushaf (the standard text; al-Suyuti's al-Itqan), in a narration in Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim, or in one an accepted grader ruled sahih (al-Albani, Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut, Ibn Hajar, al-Dhahabi) on the hadith scholars' terms (Ibn al-Salah's Muqaddima; Nukhbat al-Fikar). Hasan is not here; its place is level 3.
- level 2 [sound] — A count on the text / عدٌّ على النصّ: A tally any reader can redo on the text itself: surah and verse totals by the mushaf's numbering, a collection's hadith count by its edition's numbering, with the convention named (the counting school, with or without repetitions). Its roots are the science of verse-counting (Abu Amr al-Dani's al-Bayan fi 'add ay al-Qur'an; al-Suyuti's al-Itqan) and the hadith scholars' counts of their collections (Ibn Hajar's Hady al-Sari; al-Nawawi's introduction to Sharh Muslim).
- level 3 [sound] — In the scholars' works / في كتب العلم: Evidence the scholars act on, below level 1: a narration the named grader ruled hasan; a report the sirah and history cleared works (Ibn Hisham, al-Tabari) carry as their account of the event; an estimate the contemporary academies and committees agree on, or one body decided with no known dissent; or the majority of jurists' position on a measure (the Kuwaiti Fiqh Encyclopaedia, al-Mughni, al-Majmu'). The bodies' agreement is agreement among contemporaries, not ijma', so it sits here, not at level 1.
- level 4 [neutral] — One of several figures / رقمٌ من أرقام: A recognised difference, not a strength: the sirah and history works give several figures and adopt none; the bodies differ; the figure is one school's adopted position on a matter the schools differ over; or a report from the Israiliyyat that is neither affirmed nor denied (Ibn Taymiyya's third kind, in Muqaddima fi usul al-tafsir). The colour marks the difference; it neither prefers nor weakens.
- level 5 [weak] — A weak report / خبرٌ ضعيف: The named grader's ruling of weak (da'if) on the hadith scholars' terms; a report one work carries from one reporter with a weak chain and does not adopt; or a view within a school that the school does not adopt.
- level 6 [corrected] — Fabricated / موضوع: The named grader's ruling of fabrication (Ibn al-Salah's Muqaddima: al-mawdu'; Ibn al-Jawzi's al-Mawdu'at): the figure is not from the text at all. The bottom rung; its colour is the corrected colour because the ruling itself is a correction. Whether it is published under a video is an open question the owner decides.
### no_origin
- level 0 [notfound] — No source / لا مصدر: No source was found for the part, so there is nothing to rank; this is where this kind's exits normally sit. It has no colour of its own: the colour comes from the state (not_found takes the not-found colour; pending, rerouted, not a claim and not checked take neutral).
- level 1 [strong] — The rule in a text or the scholar's own book / القاعدة في نصٍّ أو في كتاب العالم: The rule the speaker's case would fall under is stated in a closed text (a verse; a hadith in the Sahihayn or graded sahih by an accepted grader) or a consensus documented from its books, or it is the scholar's own words in his own book. The case itself is not stated there.
- level 2 [sound] — The rule carried by a cleared work / القاعدة في مصدر معتمد: The rule is carried by a cleared fiqh work, stated by a cleared fatwa body with no known dissent or by the bodies agreeing, or it is the majority view. The case itself is not stated.
- level 3 [neutral] — The rule itself is disputed / القاعدة نفسها محل خلاف: The rule the speaker built on is itself a recognised difference between the schools or the bodies, or one school's adopted position. The case itself is not stated.
- level 4 [weak] — The rule rests on a weak view / القاعدة على قول ضعيف: The rule rests on a narration an accepted grader ruled weak, a non-adopted view within its school, a view outside the four schools, or a fatwa its body later revised. The case itself is not stated.
